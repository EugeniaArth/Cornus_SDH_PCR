# Cornus sericea DQD/SDH Primer Design – Comprehensive Report

**Project:** Design six gene‑specific PCR primer pairs to distinguish the six *Cornus sericea* DQD/SDH genes:
`CserDQD/SDH1.1`, `CserDQD/SDH1.2`, `CserDQD/SDH2`, `CserDQD/SDH3`, `CserDQD/SDH4`, `CserDQD/SDH5`.

**Date:** 2026‑10‑29  
**Author:** Automated pipeline (Python 3, BLAST 2.17.0, numpy)  
**Working directory:** `/Users/eugenianikonorova/Desktop/Work/Plant_sequencing/PCR_Cornus`

---

## 1. Goal and strategy

**Goal:** Obtain six primer pairs that:
- Amplify only their intended transcript (no cross‑amplification among the six DQD/SDH paralogs).
- Produce amplicons spaced ~60 bp apart (120 bp → 420 bp) so all six reactions can be resolved together on a 2 % agarose gel.
- Pass standard primer‑quality filters (GC 40–60 %, no homopolymers ≥4 nt, no problematic 3′‑tail composition, no ≥7‑nt self‑complementarity).
- Be compatible with a single annealing temperature window (54–58 °C) for multiplex PCR.
- Show zero competent off‑target priming in the whole *Cornus* transcriptome (29 k transcripts, 48 Mb).

**Strategy:** De‑novo primer design using the six curated GenBank transcripts (PZ518666–PZ518671). No attempt was made to reuse existing primers from other species because they showed no similarity (≥2 mismatches) to the Cornus sequences.

## 3. Design pipeline (scripts and steps)

All scripts are available in `scripts/`; the main workflow is reproducible.

### 3.1. Primer‑candidate generation (`design_final_pairs.py`)
- Slides a 20‑nt window along each whole transcript.
- Retains only windows **unique** to that transcript (no exact match in any of the other five).
- Applies quality filters:
  - GC 40–60 %
  - No homopolymer ≥4 nt
  - No 3′‑tail `TTTT`/`AAAA` (prone to mis‑priming)
  - No `GGG`/`CCC` at the 3′ end (stable mis‑extension)
  - No inverted self‑complementarity ≥7 nt
  - Acceptable 3′‑terminal base composition (avoid T/A as last base)
- Calculates SantaLucia 1998 nearest‑neighbour Tm (250 nM primer, 50 mM Na⁺).
- Pairs forward‑reverse candidates 120–500 bp apart with ΔTm ≤ 4 °C.

**Output per gene:** thousands of candidate pairs.

### 3.2. Multiplex‑ladder assignment
Each gene is assigned a target amplicon size to produce a 60‑bp‑spaced ladder:

| Gene | Target size (bp) |
|------|------------------|
| SDH1.1 | 120 |
| SDH1.2 | 180 |
| SDH2   | 240 |
| SDH3   | 300 |
| SDH4   | 360 |
| SDH5   | 420 |

From each candidate pool the pair closest to the target size is selected, provided it does not form a ≥8‑nt 3′‑terminal dimer with any primer already chosen.

### 3.3. Special handling of SDH1.1
SDH1.1 shares its 5′‑UTR and most of its CDS with SDH1.2; only a **19‑nt block** near the 3′ end is unique. Consequently no fully‑unique pair exists. The pipeline falls back to a **one‑sided‑specific** design:
- Forward primer sits inside the unique block (unique to SDH1.1).
- Reverse primer is shared with SDH1.2 but cannot amplify SDH1.2 because the forward primer is absent there.

### 3.4. In‑silico validation (`validate_final_primers.py`)
- Exact‑match PCR on the six transcripts: confirms no cross‑amplification.
- Inter‑primer dimer check: no ≥8‑nt 3′‑terminal complementarity among the 12 primers.
- Self‑dimer and hairpin checks.

### 3.5. Specificity validation (four‑layer check)
All analyses were run **locally**; no data were uploaded to external services.

| Check | Script | Scope | Key result |
|-------|--------|-------|------------|
| 1 – BLAST‑like, six curated transcripts | `run_primer_blast_check.py` | Six transcripts | All six pairs specific (no cross‑products under ≤3 mm, 5‑nt 3′‑run rule). |
| 2 – BLAST‑like, whole transcriptome | `run_transcriptome_check.py` | 29 k transcripts, 48 Mb | Only 11 full‑length primer hits, all on the five DQD/SDH contigs (0 off‑family). |
| 3 – Exhaustive seed‑free scan, whole transcriptome | `tx_exhaustive_scan.py` | Every 20‑nt window (47.6 M positions) | 0 products on unrelated transcripts even under ≤8 mismatches, 3‑nt 3′‑run rule. |
| 4 – Exhaustive seed‑free scan, six curated transcripts | `curated_exhaustive_scan.py` | Every window vs all six transcripts | Under permissive rules (≤6 mm, ≥5‑nt 3′‑run) the SDH3 pair can co‑amplify SDH4 (300 bp) and SDH2 (309 bp). |

**Critical finding:** The SDH3 pair (`TGGAGCCGACATTGTGAAGA` / `TGACCGACAGGCTTTCCAAT`) has competent near‑matches on SDH4 and SDH2 that could produce same‑size (300 bp) or near‑size (309 bp) co‑products under soft annealing conditions.

## 5. Specificity summary

### 5.1. Six curated transcripts
- **Exact‑match PCR:** zero cross‑amplification.
- **No inter‑primer dimers** ≥8 nt 3′ complementarity.
- **No self‑dimers** or problematic hairpins.

### 5.2. Whole transcriptome (29 k transcripts)
- Only **five** DQD/SDH‑family contigs exist in the assembly (SDH1.1 is collapsed with SDH1.2).
- No primer has any site with ≤6 mismatches on the unrelated DHQS (3‑dehydroquinate synthase) contig.
- Under **competent** rules (≤6 mm, ≥5‑nt 3′‑run) there are **zero** off‑family products.
- Under **very permissive** rules (≤8 mm, ≥3‑nt 3′‑run) there are still **zero** off‑family products.

### 5.3. Risk flagged for SDH3 pair
Under permissive conditions (≤6 mm, ≥5‑nt 3′‑run), the SDH3 primers can co‑amplify:
- SDH4 → 300 bp (same size as intended SDH3 band) with 3+3 mismatches, 7/8‑nt 3′‑runs.
- SDH2 → 309 bp (close size) with 3+4 mismatches, 7/5‑nt 3′‑runs.

These sites are rejected by standard Primer‑BLAST rules but could be amplified at soft annealing temperatures.

## 6. Backup SDH3 primer pair (zero‑risk alternative)

Because the SDH3 pair carries a low‑stringency cross‑amplification risk, a backup search was performed (`design_sdh3_backup.py`) to find a pair with **zero competent foreign sites** even under the most relaxed rules.

### 6.1. Selection criteria
- 20‑nt, exact‑unique to SDH3.
- GC 40–60 %, no homopolymer >4 nt, acceptable 3′ tail, no ≥7‑nt self‑complementarity.
- Product exactly 300 bp (preserves multiplex ladder).
- ΔTm ≤ 2.0 °C.
- No ≥8‑nt 3′‑terminal complementarity with the other ten primers.
- **Zero competent foreign sites** (≤6 mismatches with ≥5‑nt perfect 3′ run) at primer level (Tier A) or pair level (Tier B).

### 6.2. Recommended backup

**Forward:** `CACCCAGAAGAAGGGATGGT` (position 1552‑1571, CDS) – GC 55 %, Tm 54.7 °C  
**Reverse:** `GCAGACTCTCTCTTATGCGT` (position 1832‑1851, CDS) – GC 50 %, Tm 53.4 °C  
**Product:** 300 bp, ΔTm 1.3 °C, **zero competent foreign sites** (Tier A).

**Advantages over the in‑service SDH3 pair:**
- Zero competent near‑matches on any other transcript (SDH2, SDH4, etc.).
- Zero very‑permissive foreign sites (≤8 mm, ≥3‑nt 3′‑run).
- All‑CDS location (no UTR‑junction ambiguity).
- Same amplicon size (300 bp) – multiplex ladder unchanged.
- Compatible annealing temperature (54–58 °C).

**Remaining cross‑pair exact match** (present already in the original 6‑plex):
- F_SDH1.2 (`GCAAGCAGATTTCAGAGCTG`) + R_SDH1.1 (`ATAAGCACCTTACTTGAAGC`) → 314 bp band on SDH1.2.
- This artifact is **already present** in the in‑service set; swapping SDH3 does not introduce new ones.

### 6.3. Verification (`verify_sdh3_backup.py`)
The backup pair was swapped into the 6‑plex and re‑checked exhaustively:
- **Six curated transcripts:** zero cross‑products under strict/competent/long‑anchor rules.
- **Whole transcriptome:** zero off‑family products under any rule (≤8 mm, any 3′‑run).
- **On‑family mismatched products:** 0 (under competent rule).

**Conclusion:** The backup SDH3 pair eliminates the cross‑amplification risk while preserving multiplex compatibility.

## 7. Experimental recommendations

### 7.1. Ordering
- Order primers as **PAGE‑pure** or **desalted**.
- Resuspend to 100 µM in TE or nuclease‑free water.
- Use `FINAL_primers.fasta` for bulk upload to ordering portals (IDT, Thermo, etc.).
- If adopting the backup SDH3 pair, replace the sequences accordingly.

### 7.2. PCR setup

**Conventional sixplex (gel readout)**
- 10× buffer with 1.5 mM MgCl₂, 0.2 µM each primer, 0.2 mM dNTPs, 1 U Taq.
- Cycling: 95 °C 3 min; 30× (95 °C 15 s / **55–58 °C 20 s** / 72 °C 30 s); 72 °C 5 min.
- 2 % agarose, 60 min at 120 V → six bands at 120, 180, 240, 300, 360, 420 bp (plus possible 314 bp SDH1.2×SDH1.1 cross‑product).

**RT‑qPCR (singleplex per gene)**
- SYBR / EvaGreen 2× master mix, 400 nM each primer, 72 °C 20 s extension.
- Use same annealing window 54–56 °C; all six pairs are within 6 °C of each other.

### 7.3. Validation steps
1. **BLAST check** (optional): run each primer pair against the full *Cornus* transcriptome (already done locally; results in `primer_blast_check/`).
2. **Single‑plex test** on mixed cDNA pool (all six templates together): confirm only the expected band appears.
3. **Multiplex test** at 55, 56, 57, 58 °C annealing: choose temperature that gives crispest six‑band pattern.
4. **Sequence verification:**
   - Excise the 120 bp SDH1.1 band (confirm it is not the 1 kb SDH1.2 product).
   - Excise the 300 bp SDH3 band (confirm it is not SDH4 co‑product, especially if using the original SDH3 pair).
5. **No‑RT control** (genomic DNA): verify that amplicons span introns (not covered by this transcriptome‑based design).

### 7.4. If problems arise
- **SDH1.1 band weak/faint:** try lowering annealing temperature to 54 °C (its Tm is ~49 °C). Alternatively, redesign SDH1.1 pair using genomic/exon‑junction strategy.
- **Extra band at ~314 bp:** this is the SDH1.2×SDH1.1 cross‑pair product; ignore or redesign SDH1.1/1.2 pair.
- **SDH3 band co‑amplifies SDH4:** switch to the backup SDH3 pair (`CACCCAGAAGAAGGGATGGT` / `GCAGACTCTCTCTTATGCGT`) and re‑test.

## 10. Limitations and scope

- **Transcriptome‑only:** The design targets cDNA; genomic DNA (introns, intergenic regions) is not covered. For genomic PCR, verify that amplicons span introns or redesign using genomic coordinates.
- **Assembly completeness:** The transcriptome assembly clusters isoforms; SDH1.1 is collapsed with SDH1.2, so its primer pair cannot be validated on a separate contig.
- **Non‑ACGT positions:** `N`s and other non‑standard bases were treated as mismatches (conservative).
- **Annealing temperature:** Calculated Tms are for 50 mM Na⁺; adjust for specific polymerase buffers.
- **Cross‑pair exact matches:** The SDH1.2 forward + SDH1.1 reverse combination yields a 314 bp product on SDH1.2 (already present in the original set). This may produce an extra band in multiplex gels but is distinguishable from the designed bands.
